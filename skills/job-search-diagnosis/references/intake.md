# Intake

Ask in one message, as a short numbered list the user can answer inline. Skip anything they already gave.

## Required
1. **Target role(s)** — titles you're applying for (e.g. "SWE new grad", "data analyst", "product designer").
2. **Funnel counts so far** (rough is fine):
   - Applications sent
   - Any response (OA, recruiter email/call, or "next steps")
   - First-round / phone / recruiter screens done
   - Onsites / final rounds done
   - Offers
3. **How long** you've been actively applying (weeks).

## Helpful
4. Graduation date and degree/major.
5. Work authorization: US citizen/GC, F-1 OPT, STEM OPT, or needs H-1B sponsorship now/later.
6. Channel mix: roughly what share came through referrals, career fair/recruiter outreach, or cold online applications.
7. Locations / remote preference.
8. Resume (paste the text) and 2–3 JDs you really want.
9. For Stage 2+: the questions you got and where you felt it went wrong, plus any feedback you received.

## Missing data
- No counts at all: ask for them once. If the user still can't give them, ask them to estimate from their email/ATS
  inbox ("search for 'application received' and 'next steps'"). Don't diagnose a stage without at least the
  application and response counts.
- Counts but no resume: diagnose the stage from the numbers. Offer a resume check only if the stage is 1.
- Mixed roles: if they apply to very different roles (e.g. SWE + PM + consulting), compute the funnel per role if
  they can split it. Spread-out direction is often the finding.
