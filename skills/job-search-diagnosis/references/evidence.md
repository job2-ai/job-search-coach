# Evidence behind the coaching

Every rule the coach follows, and the research it rests on. We read each source ourselves (full text or abstract),
in November 2026.

**Strength of evidence:**
- **meta** = meta-analysis
- **RCT** = randomized trial
- **field** = field experiment
- **platform** = a hiring vendor's data from its own platform (large samples, but not peer-reviewed)
- **heuristic** = no published data

When you cite research to the user, keep it to one plain sentence ("Research on job-search programs found…"). Don't
lecture, and never quote a number that isn't on this page.

## The funnel benchmarks

| Step | What the data says | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Cold application → positive response (assessment or interview invite) | Application → interview: 3.6% (technical) and 4.7% (business) in Q1 2026 (Ashby, 109M applications). Application → first screen: 6% for inbound in 2024 (Gem 2025 Recruiting Benchmarks, 140M+ applications). Callbacks to online postings: 4.7% (Kroft, Lange & Notowidigdo 2013, *QJE* 128(3); ~12,000 résumés in 100 US metro areas). Entry-level jobs at large employers applied through their own career sites: ~24% contacted within 30 days (Kline, Rose & Walters 2022, *QJE* 137(4)). | platform + field |
| Referred application → interview | Referred candidates reach interview at 40% vs 3% for inbound (Ashby referrals report, 38M applications). At one US firm, referrals were 6% of applicants but 21% of interviewees and 29% of hires, with more than twice the interview odds of job-board applicants (6.0% baseline) (Brown, Setren & Topa 2016, *J Labor Economics* 34(1), doi:10.1086/682338). | platform + field |
| Response → first round | Nobody measures this. Applicant-tracking data never sees rejection emails. | heuristic |
| First round → final round | 20% for inbound candidates in 2024, 29% in 2021 (Gem 2025). | platform |
| Final round → offer | 35% for inbound candidates in 2024, 48% in 2021 (Gem 2025). At one firm, 32% of interviewed job-board applicants got offers (Brown, Setren & Topa 2016). | platform + field |
| Whole funnel | About 0.5% of applications become hires (Gem 2025). 291 applications per hire (Ashby 2026). | platform |

Rates fell from 2021 to 2024–26, so the market really did get harder. Say this when someone blames themselves.

**Not supported, so don't use:** any number for how much needing visa sponsorship lowers callbacks. No US field
experiment varies sponsorship. Be honest that it's a real barrier, and check the person's targeting. Don't quote a
penalty.

## Coaching rules and their evidence

1. **Pair a skill with a motivation step in every plan.** Across 47 job-search interventions, participants had
   2.67 times the odds of getting a job, but only programs that combined skill development with motivation
   enhancement improved employment. Six components helped: job-search skills, self-presentation, self-efficacy,
   proactivity, goal setting, and social support. (Liu, Huang & Wang 2014, *Psychological Bulletin* 140(4),
   doi:10.1037/a0035923; meta)
2. **Weight effort toward active employer contact.** Applying and contacting employers correlates with interviews
   at rc = .44; preparation alone only at .19. (Van Hooft, Kammeyer-Mueller, Wanberg, Kanfer & Basbug 2021,
   *J Applied Psychology* 106(5), doi:10.1037/apl0000675; meta, 378 samples, N = 165,933)
3. **Fix quality and targeting before adding volume.** Search intensity barely relates to the quality of the job
   found (rc = .06). Search quality (.19) and goal clarity (.12) do. Job-search anxiety relates to lower search
   quality (−.24). (Van Hooft et al. 2021; meta)
4. **Build confidence, because it drives effort.** Job-search self-efficacy relates to search behavior
   (rc = .27–.30). (Kanfer, Wanberg & Kantrowitz 2001, *J Applied Psychology* 86(5),
   doi:10.1037/0021-9010.86.5.837; Van Hooft et al. 2021; meta)
5. **End with an if-then plan** (what, when, where). Implementation intentions carried job seekers from intending
   to search to actually searching. (Van Hooft, Born, Taris, van der Flier & Blonk 2005, *J Vocational Behavior*
   66(2), doi:10.1016/j.jvb.2004.10.003; N = 175) Expect a modest effect, not a miracle.
6. **Frame each week as an experiment, not a test.** In a field experiment, a learning-goal focus raised search
   behavior and reemployment. (Van Hooft & Noordzij 2009, *J Applied Psychology* 94(6), doi:10.1037/a0017592)
7. **Prepare for rejection before it comes.** "Inoculation against setbacks" is a core part of the JOBS program.
   Its randomized trials improved reemployment quality, and at two years improved reemployment and income and
   reduced depressive symptoms, most for people who started discouraged. (Caplan, Vinokur, Price & van Ryn 1989,
   *J Applied Psychology* 74(5), N = 928; Vinokur, Schul, Vuori & Price 2000, *J Occupational Health Psychology*
   5(1); RCT)
8. **Track inputs they control, and keep it going weekly.** Daily mood and confidence follow perceived progress.
   (Wanberg, Zhu & Van Hooft 2010, *Academy of Management Journal* 53(4); N = 233, daily surveys) Search
   intensity tends to drop over the weeks, and people who sustained it got interviews and jobs sooner. (Wanberg,
   Zhu, Kanfer & Zhang 2012, *AMJ* 55(2), doi:10.5465/amj.2010.0157; N = 177, 20 weeks)
9. **Prescribe referrals when applications get no interviews.** See the referral row in the benchmarks table.
   Moderately weak ties pass on the most jobs. (Rajkumar et al. 2022, *Science* 377,
   doi:10.1126/science.abl4476; randomized, 20M+ LinkedIn users) Referred hires were also more likely to be hired
   and to stay. (Burks, Cowgill, Hoffman & Housman 2015, *QJE* 130(2), doi:10.1093/qje/qjv010)
10. **Use the ingredients that make career counseling work.** The ingredients are written exercises, individualized
    interpretation and feedback, world-of-work information, modeling, and building support. (Brown & Ryan Krane
    2000, *Handbook of Counseling Psychology*, 3rd ed.) The conversation steps map onto them:
    - Writing the plan and the progress card → written exercises
    - Feedback on their own funnel → individualized feedback
    - The benchmarks → world-of-work information
    - A rewritten example bullet or answer → modeling
    - Asking who can help (referrals, peers, career services) → building support

## Interviews
- Structured interviews predict job performance better than unstructured ones (validity .42; Sackett, Zhang, Berry &
  Lievens 2022, *J Applied Psychology* 107(11), doi:10.1037/apl0000994). Employers increasingly score answers
  against a rubric, so structured (STAR) answers are worth practicing.
- Interview coaching was associated with higher structured-interview ratings, partly by teaching the format. (Tross
  & Maurer 2008, *J Occupational and Organizational Psychology*, doi:10.1348/096317907X248653)
- Interview anxiety relates to lower interview performance (meta-analytic r = −.19; Powell, Stanley & Brown 2018,
  *Canadian J Behavioural Science* 50(4)). Practice and preparation are the levers.
- One internship raised the interview rate by about 14% (2.2 points) in a résumé audit of ~9,400 applications.
  (Nunley, Pugh, Romero & Seals 2016, *Labour Economics* 38, doi:10.1016/j.labeco.2015.11.002)
- Long unemployment gaps lower callbacks, mostly in the first 8 months. (Kroft et al. 2013) Encourage visible
  activity, like projects, contract work or courses, as something the person controls, not as a threat.

## Wellbeing
- Distress during unemployment is common, caused by the situation, and eases on reemployment:
  - 34% of unemployed vs 16% of employed people had psychological problems (Paul & Moser 2009, *J Vocational
    Behavior* 74, doi:10.1016/j.jvb.2009.01.001; meta, 237 cross-sectional studies).
  - Financial strain is a main pathway (Wanberg 2012, *Annual Review of Psychology* 63,
    doi:10.1146/annurev-psych-120710-100500).
- Expressive writing about a job loss helped laid-off professionals find work sooner. (Spera, Buhrfeind & Pennebaker
  1994, *Academy of Management Journal* 37(3); N = 63. Don't quote percentages, because secondary sources
  conflict.)
- A self-compassion writing exercise about bad job-search experiences lowered sadness and raised calm, through less
  self-criticism. (Kreemers, van Hooft, van Vianen & Sisouw de Zilwa 2020, *Frontiers in Psychology* 11,
  doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2020.01371; N = 180)
- Asking directly about suicidal thoughts doesn't plant them. (Dazzi, Gribble, Wessely & Fear 2014, *Psychological
  Medicine* 44, doi:10.1017/S0033291714001299)
- Safe-messaging guidance: don't describe methods, don't present suicide as common or inevitable, and point toward
  help and recovery. (https://suicidepreventionmessaging.org)

## International students
- Acculturative stress correlates with depression at r = .41. (Amlashi, Majzoobi & Forstmeier 2024, *Frontiers in
  Psychology* 15, doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1403807; meta, 26 studies, N = 7,247)
- Acculturative distress predicted lower career outcome expectations. (Reynolds & Constantine 2007, *J Career
  Assessment* 15(3), doi:10.1177/1069072707301218; N = 261)
- In interviews with F-1 students, visa uncertainty was its own stressor:
  - All 12 worried the 12-month OPT was too short.
  - 10 of 12 were stressed about how scarce H-1B sponsorship is.
  - Contingency plans, peer information-sharing and social support helped.

  (Domínguez, Cheng & De La Rue 2022, *The Counseling Psychologist* 50(6), doi:10.1177/00110000221097358;
  qualitative)

## Myths we don't repeat
- "75% of résumés are rejected by ATS." It traces back to a 2012 sales pitch from a résumé-optimization vendor.
  There is no study behind it.
- "Apply within 24 hours or don't bother." No traceable source. LinkedIn says applying early can help, but hasn't
  published a method. Early applying is a reasonable heuristic, not a finding.
- Any specific "sponsorship penalty" percentage. No US field experiment measures it.
